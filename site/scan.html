What it checks

Four checks

Access for AI agents

Whether robots.txt and your server let the common assistants in, and whether key pages show their text without running JavaScript.

Visible pricing

Whether a buyer's assistant can find a number, or at least a starting price and what it depends on.

The facts buyers ask for

Integrations, security reports, trial terms, deployment and limits: stated plainly, on a page that loads.

Machine-readable signals

An llms.txt file, structured data, a sitemap, and clear page titles that help an assistant find the right page.
