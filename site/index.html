Questions

Short answers

What does an assistant actually do when it evaluates you?

It takes the buyer's request, searches, and opens a handful of pages on your site: usually the home page, pricing, integrations, security and docs. It tries to answer each question from what it finds. If a fact is not stated plainly on a page it can load, it says it could not confirm it, or guesses, and moves on to the next vendor.

What do you read, and what do you never do?

We read public pages only, at a polite rate, and we obey robots.txt. We never log in, never submit forms, and never contact your team or your customers.

How accurate is it?

A report is a snapshot: what specific assistants found on a specific day. Assistants change, and the same question can get a different answer on another run. We show the evidence for every answer so you can check it yourself. See the method for the limits.
