Privacy

What we collect.

Two things, and only if you give them to us.

What we keep

  • The domain you type into the scan, so we can scan that site.
  • Your email address, if you join the waiting list, so we can write to you about it.

Nothing else. No name, no company details, no phone number.

What we do not do

  • No trackers, analytics scripts, advertising pixels or cookies on this site.
  • No fonts, scripts or images loaded from other companies' servers.
  • We do not sell or share your email. We do not add you to other lists.

The sites we scan

The scan reads public pages of the domain you enter, at a polite rate, obeying robots.txt. It does not log in or submit forms.

Removing your data

Ask us and we will delete your email and any domains you submitted.

Contact: contact address to be added

Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time for security. We do not use them for anything else.
